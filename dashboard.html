How It Works

Wealthspring is a private capital facility offering short-cycle allocations. Simple onboarding, transparent accrual, and complete liquidity — your capital remains accessible at all times.

01

Allocate & Fund

Select your allocation tier — Bronze to Platinum. Fund via your dedicated deposit address: BTC Native or USDT-BEP20. Submit your transaction hash. Deposits are reflected as Pending immediately and confirmed within 5–10 minutes upon on-chain verification.

02

Capital at Work

Once confirmed, your allocation enters an active 7-day cycle. Returns accrue daily at 10%–22% depending on tier and compound automatically. Track performance and total return in real time from your Overview.

03

Liquidity On Demand

Wealthspring operates with full liquidity. Withdraw your principal and accrued returns at any time with no fees or penalties. Upon completion of your 7-day cycle, profits are fully settled. Early withdrawals are processed immediately at the full requested amount — you retain complete control.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the allocation limits?
Minimum initial allocation is $100. Maximum per transaction is $9,999. You may hold multiple active allocations simultaneously — no cap on total portfolio size.
Is liquidity restricted? Can I withdraw at any time?
No. Wealthspring is a fully liquid facility. You may withdraw your principal and accrued returns at any time, with no fees, no penalties, and no approval delays. Your 7-day cycle defines the profit settlement period — it does not restrict access to your capital. Full control remains with you at all times.
How long does deposit confirmation take?
All deposits appear as Pending instantly upon submission. On-chain confirmation and admin verification are completed within 5–10 minutes, after which your Available Balance is updated.
Which assets and networks are supported?
We support Bitcoin (Native BTC) and Tether USD on BNB Smart Chain (USDT-BEP20). This ensures low fees and rapid settlement.
How is client support handled?
All inquiries submitted via Support • Live are logged with a ticket ID, stored to your account history, and forwarded directly to operations. Average first response is under 15 minutes.
How are returns calculated?
Returns are fixed by tier: Bronze 10%, Silver 15%, Gold 18%, Platinum 22% per 7-day cycle. Returns accrue daily and compound automatically if not withdrawn.
